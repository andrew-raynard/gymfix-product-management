# GymFix — Product Roadmap

**Phase:** 8 — Product Roadmap & Learnings  
**Status:** Proposed roadmap  
**Planning Approach:** Now / Next / Later

## 1. Roadmap Objective

Guide GymFix's development beyond the initial browser-based prototype while prioritising validation, operational reliability, and demonstrated customer value.

This roadmap represents proposed directions rather than committed releases.

## 2. NOW — Validate the MVP

**Goal:** Determine whether GymFix's core workflow is usable and addresses meaningful customer problems.

Initiatives:
- Conduct real usability testing with gym members.
- Validate maintenance workflows with gym operators.
- Investigate reporting friction and repair visibility.
- Verify complete ticket lifecycle behaviour.
- Establish baseline usability metrics.

Success criteria:
- Users can complete critical tasks independently.
- Major usability blockers are identified.
- Research clarifies whether the proposed product addresses an important problem.

## 3. NEXT — Improve Operational Readiness

**Goal:** Prepare GymFix for a potential real-world pilot.

Candidate initiatives:
- Shared database persistence.
- Real authentication and role-based permissions.
- Equipment maintenance history.
- Improved ticket reference retrieval.
- Audit trails for maintenance actions.
- Stronger error handling and data validation.

Dependencies:
- Evidence of operator interest.
- Technical feasibility assessment.
- Data privacy and security requirements.

## 4. LATER — Explore Expansion

**Goal:** Extend the product if initial adoption and customer value are demonstrated.

Potential initiatives:
- Multi-branch management.
- Member notifications.
- Operational analytics.
- Preventive maintenance scheduling.
- External maintenance integrations.

AI-powered diagnostics and predictive maintenance remain exploratory due to their complexity and data requirements.

## 5. Roadmap Trade-offs

| Decision | Rationale |
|---|---|
| Validate before expanding | Avoid investing in unproven needs |
| Prioritise shared persistence | Required for real multi-user operations |
| Defer predictive AI | Limited evidence and historical data |
| Defer advanced analytics | Requires reliable operational data |
| Consider notifications later | Additional complexity and unvalidated demand |

## 6. Roadmap Risks

- Operators may already have adequate maintenance solutions.
- The product may not provide sufficient differentiation.
- Staff may resist additional administrative work.
- Software cannot independently solve spare-part or technician shortages.
- A working demo may not translate into real adoption.

## 7. Roadmap Review Criteria

Priorities should be revisited when new evidence emerges about:
- User needs.
- Product usability.
- Operational effectiveness.
- Technical feasibility.
- Commercial demand.

## 8. Conclusion

GymFix's next development stage should prioritise validating the core product opportunity and improving operational readiness before expanding its feature set.

The roadmap remains flexible and evidence-dependent.
