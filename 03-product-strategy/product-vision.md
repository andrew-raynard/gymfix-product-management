# GymFix — Product Vision & Strategy

**Project:** GymFix — Gym Equipment Maintenance Management System  
**Phase:** 3 — Product Strategy  
**Date:** October 2026  
**Status:** Proposed Strategy — Validation Ongoing

## 1. Product Vision

Make gym equipment maintenance transparent, accountable, and easy to manage, so members can spend less time dealing with broken equipment and more time training.

## 2. Product Mission

Help gym operators capture equipment faults, coordinate maintenance, and communicate repair progress through one accessible platform.

## 3. Problem Opportunity

Secondary research identified examples of prolonged equipment downtime, uncertainty about repair progress, and operational constraints affecting maintenance.

Existing maintenance products and low-cost reporting alternatives already address parts of this problem.

GymFix therefore explores whether a lightweight, end-to-end maintenance workflow can provide sufficient value for smaller gym operators.

## 4. Target Users

**Target customer:** Small and medium-sized gym operators (provisional segment).

**Primary operational user:** Gym manager / branch staff.

**Primary reporting user:** Gym member.

**Supporting user:** Maintenance technician.

These segments are hypotheses informed by Phase 1 and Phase 2 research.

## 5. Strategic Objectives

### Objective 1 — Improve Maintenance Accountability

Ensure equipment faults are recorded, assigned, and tracked through resolution.

### Objective 2 — Improve Repair Visibility

Enable staff and members to understand maintenance status without repeated manual enquiries.

### Objective 3 — Reduce Administrative Friction

Simplify reporting, assignment, and status updates.

## 6. Proposed Pilot OKRs

| Objective | Key Result | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Accountability | Tickets assigned within 4 hours | >= 90% |
| Accountability | Tickets with an assigned owner | >= 95% |
| Visibility | Successful ticket status lookup | >= 90% |
| Efficiency | Median report submission time | <= 60 seconds |
| Efficiency | Reporting task completion rate | >= 90% |

These are proposed targets, not actual results. Operational targets require a real pilot.

## 7. Product Principles

1. Simplicity before feature breadth.
2. Accountability across the full maintenance workflow.
3. Clear and accurate status communication.
4. Low administrative overhead.
5. Evidence-based prioritisation.

## 8. Strategic Trade-offs

### Prioritise

- Equipment fault reporting.
- Centralised ticket management.
- Staff assignment.
- Maintenance status tracking.
- Repair blocker documentation.

### Defer

- AI-powered fault diagnosis.
- Predictive maintenance.
- Advanced enterprise analytics.
- Subscription billing.
- Complex multi-organisation administration.

## 9. Risks and Assumptions

- Smaller gym operators may already have adequate tools.
- Staff may not consistently update repair statuses.
- Software cannot independently resolve technician or spare-part shortages.
- Users may not adopt an additional reporting interface.
- Willingness to pay has not been established.
