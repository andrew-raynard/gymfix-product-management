# GymFix — Evidence-Informed User Personas

**Phase:** 2 — User Research
**Date:** October 2026
**Status:** Provisional / Requires Primary Validation

## Research Approach

These role-based proto-personas were developed using secondary research from public gym-member and gym-operator discussions, alongside assumptions about maintenance workflows.

They are not representations of interviewed individuals or statistically validated user segments.

See [Phase 1 Research](../01-problem-discovery/discovery-report.md) and the accompanying evidence log for supporting sources.

## Persona 1 — Gym Member

**Role:** Equipment user and potential fault reporter

### Goals
- Complete workouts safely and efficiently.
- Report equipment faults without unnecessary disruption.
- Understand whether faulty equipment is available again.

### Potential Pain Points
- Equipment unavailable for extended periods.
- Uncertainty about whether reports have been acknowledged.
- Inconvenience of repeatedly approaching staff.

### Current Alternatives
- Report verbally to staff.
- Use existing gym feedback channels.
- Avoid faulty equipment or change workout plans.

### Candidate JTBD
When I encounter faulty gym equipment, I want to report it quickly and know whether the issue is being addressed, so I can continue exercising safely and plan my workouts.

### Evidence Confidence
Moderate evidence for equipment downtime; limited direct evidence for reporting friction.

## Persona 2 — Gym Manager / Branch Staff

**Role:** Maintenance coordination and operational oversight

### Goals
- Keep equipment safe and available.
- Track unresolved maintenance issues.
- Coordinate repairs efficiently.

### Potential Pain Points
- Fault reports arriving through different channels.
- Difficulty identifying outstanding maintenance work.
- Repair delays involving technicians or parts.
- Repeated member enquiries.

### Current Alternatives
- Verbal communication.
- Messaging applications.
- Google Forms and spreadsheets.
- Dedicated maintenance management software.

### Candidate JTBD
When an equipment fault is reported, I want to track ownership, progress, and blockers, so I can coordinate repairs and maintain operational visibility.

### Evidence Confidence
Some direct evidence for existing reporting alternatives and repair constraints; limited evidence for specific internal workflows.

## Persona 3 — Maintenance Technician

**Role:** Equipment diagnosis and repair

### Goals
- Receive accurate fault information.
- Complete repairs efficiently.
- Communicate repair blockers and progress.

### Potential Pain Points
- Incomplete equipment information.
- Unclear fault descriptions.
- Replacement-part delays.
- Coordination across multiple stakeholders.

### Current Alternatives
- Work orders.
- Phone calls and messaging applications.
- Existing service management systems.

### Candidate JTBD
When I receive a maintenance assignment, I want accurate equipment details and a way to communicate progress, so I can diagnose and resolve faults efficiently.

### Evidence Confidence
Limited. Technician workflows require further validation.

## Persona Prioritisation

**Primary operational persona:** Gym Manager / Branch Staff

**Primary reporting persona:** Gym Member

**Supporting persona:** Maintenance Technician

This prioritisation is provisional. The MVP must create value for both gym operators and members.

## Validation Gaps

1. Do gym members want direct repair notifications?
2. How do smaller gym operators currently manage faults?
3. How frequently do staff lose track of maintenance requests?
4. What information do technicians actually need?
5. Would a dedicated system outperform existing low-cost alternatives?

## Research Integrity

No demographic details, fictional interviews, or fabricated user quotes have been used.

These personas should be revised when new evidence becomes available.
