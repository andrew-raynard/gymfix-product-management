# GymFix — Phase 7: Simulated Usability Evaluation

**Project:** GymFix  
**Phase:** 7 — Usability Testing & Iteration  
**Status:** Completed as a simulation  
**Prototype:** https://gymfix-demo.lovable.app/  
**Method:** Hypothetical task-based usability simulation

> **Research disclosure:** This document is a simulated usability evaluation created for an independent Product Management learning project. No participants were recruited or interviewed. All participant outcomes, task timings, and usability findings in this simulation are hypothetical and must not be interpreted as observed research data.

## 1. Objective

Demonstrate how a Product Manager could evaluate GymFix's usability, measure performance against predefined KPIs, identify usability issues, and prioritise improvements.

## 2. Methodology

The exercise models five hypothetical participants attempting four tasks:

1. Submit an equipment fault report.
2. Retrieve and understand a maintenance ticket's status.
3. Assign a maintenance ticket to a technician.
4. Update the progress of an assigned repair.

The simulation assumes participants attempt the tasks independently without guidance.

## 3. Simulated Results

| Task | Hypothetical Success | Proposed KPI | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fault reporting | 5/5 (100%) | 90% | Meets target |
| Status lookup | 4/5 (80%) | 90% | Below target |
| Technician assignment | 4/5 (80%) | 90% | Below target |
| Repair status update | 5/5 (100%) | 90% | Meets target |

These are invented scenario outcomes used to demonstrate metric interpretation.

The sample size would also be too small to support population-level conclusions, even if the results were observed.

## 4. Hypothetical Usability Findings

### Finding 1 — Ticket Reference Retrieval

Scenario: A participant forgets the ticket reference and struggles to locate the previously submitted report.

Potential impact: Reduced success in checking maintenance status.

Proposed severity: P1.

### Finding 2 — Assignment Discoverability

Scenario: A participant struggles to locate the technician assignment action.

Potential impact: Longer task completion time and potential assignment errors.

Proposed severity: P2.

### Finding 3 — Status Comprehension

Scenario: A participant is unsure what a maintenance status means.

Potential impact: Confusion about whether equipment is safe or available.

Proposed severity: P2.

## 5. Proposed Iteration Backlog

| Priority | Improvement | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | Improve ticket reference visibility and retrieval guidance | Prioritise for validation |
| P2 | Make assignment actions easier to discover | Prioritise for validation |
| P2 | Add explanatory text for repair statuses | Consider |
| P3 | Automatic notifications | Defer |

## 6. Product Trade-offs

The proposed improvements focus on usability within the existing MVP rather than expanding feature scope.

Automatic notifications remain deferred because the initial product strategy prioritises lightweight reporting and maintenance coordination.

## 7. Existing Functional Validation

Separate from this simulation, the prototype underwent seven manual functional checks performed by the project creator.

All seven checks passed, covering report submission, dashboard visibility, technician assignment, navigation, status updates, member tracking, and persistence after browser refresh.

These functional checks do not constitute participant usability testing.

## 8. Limitations

- No real participants were recruited.
- All simulated results are hypothetical.
- No task completion times were measured.
- No usability improvements were implemented as part of this exercise.
- No statistical conclusions or claims of real user satisfaction can be made.

## 9. Next Steps

- Conduct real usability testing if the project progresses beyond the portfolio demonstration.
- Validate whether the hypothetical usability issues occur in practice.
- Prioritise actual observed findings over simulated scenarios.
- Revisit the MVP roadmap based on real evidence.

## 10. Conclusion

This simulation demonstrates a structured approach to usability evaluation, KPI interpretation, severity classification, and iteration planning.

It does not establish that GymFix has achieved its usability targets or that the proposed improvements are necessary.

The project can proceed to Phase 8: Product Roadmap & Learnings with these limitations documented.
